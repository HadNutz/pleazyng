# Public libraries

Open **Files & books → Public libraries** to search Open Library or Google Books, or to save a link to your library’s own website.

- **Open Library** searches the public catalogue. Open a title to see reading and borrowing options on its Open Library page. Search results do not verify local-library holdings.
- **Google Books** searches the public catalogue and shows an official purchase link when Google supplies one for your region.
- **Hoopla, The Palace Project, cloudLibrary, BorrowBox, Cantook, and Onleihe** provide shortcuts to their websites. You can enter your library’s specific HTTPS address, or choose **Another library** to save a custom site.

Sign in, borrow, and manage loans on the library’s website. Pleazyng stores each saved link in the active profile. Removing the link leaves loans and holds with the library.
